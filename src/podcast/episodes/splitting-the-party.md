Splitting the party gets a bad reputation, mostly because it's easy to run badly. This episode is about running it well: quick cuts between scenes, keeping the other players engaged while they wait, and when a split is actually the right call.

## Topics covered

- Why "never split the party" is advice for a specific failure mode, not a universal rule
- Our rule of thumb for how long a split scene should run before cutting away
- Keeping bored players engaged when it's not their turn
- A real example from The Salt-Iron Coast where splitting up solved a pacing problem
