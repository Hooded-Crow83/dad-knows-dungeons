The Salt-Iron Coast started as a one-shot about a haunted lighthouse and refused to end. Four sessions in, the party has more questions than answers: who's running cargo through the sea caves, why does the harbormaster flinch at the word "iron," and what exactly washed up with the last storm.

This is a low-prep sandbox — most sessions start with "what do you want to do today" and end somewhere neither of us expected. If you're looking for a campaign that leans into player-driven chaos more than a fixed plot, this is the one we point people to.
