We kicked off The Salt-Iron Coast with the classic "strange lights on the water" hook, and the party ran with it further than I expected — by the end of session one they'd already made an enemy of the harbormaster.

## What happened

The party arrived in Cael's Landing on a supply skiff and immediately noticed two things: the lighthouse hasn't been lit in six months, and nobody wants to explain why. Brindle, our halfling ranger, used to keep that light — which gave the whole table an easy in.

{% callout "tip" %}
If you want players to investigate something, give one of them a personal reason to already care about it. Brindle didn't need convincing to ask questions about "her" lighthouse — the hook did itself.
{% endcallout %}

By the end of the session, the party had:

- Talked their way past a suspicious dockworker
- Found a half-burned ledger in the lighthouse's base
- Made an enemy of the harbormaster, who very much doesn't want that ledger read

## GM notes

I went in with three possible "why is the lighthouse dark" answers and let the table's questions decide which one was true. That's a technique I lean on constantly — commit to *a* mystery, not the specific answer, until the players start pulling threads.

{% callout "note" %}
Session two picks up with the tide going out further than it should. That's not a coincidence.
{% endcallout %}
