Embers of the Hollow Crown is our shorter-form campaign — six-to-eight sessions, one clear goal, and a lot of morally gray decision-making. The crew is trying to reclaim a throne that most of the kingdom is glad to be rid of, which raises the obvious question: should they?

Currently on hiatus while we finish a Salt-Iron Coast story arc, but the crew's plans are very much still simmering in the background.
