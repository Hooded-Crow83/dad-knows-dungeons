In this episode, Patrick and Mac walk through what a session zero should actually cover — beyond "let's make characters." We talk tone, table expectations, safety tools, and the awkward-but-necessary conversation about scheduling.

## Topics covered

- Why session zero isn't just character creation
- The three questions we ask every new table
- Handling mismatched expectations before they become a problem mid-campaign
- A quick rundown of safety tools (X-card, lines and veils) and how we actually use them
