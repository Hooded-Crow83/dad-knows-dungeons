Session two of The Salt-Iron Coast delivered on the cliffhanger from last time: the unnaturally low tide exposed a set of sea caves nobody in Cael's Landing wants to admit exist.

## What happened

Sister Ren — very much not a fan of open water — ended up leading the way into the caves anyway, mostly out of spite toward the harbormaster. Inside, the party found crates stamped with a merchant's guild sigil that shouldn't be operating this far north, plus fresh boot prints that weren't theirs.

{% callout "warning" %}
We almost had a TPK here — I'd underestimated how nasty a mid-level water elemental encounter gets in a cramped, half-flooded cave. Lesson relearned: always sanity-check terrain against action economy before the session, not during it.
{% endcallout %}

## The ledger pays off

Remember the half-burned ledger from session one? The party cross-referenced the guild sigil against a name in it, and it turns out the harbormaster isn't the smuggler — he's covering for someone. That reframes basically everything about how the party's been treating him.

## GM notes

This is the session where a "sandbox" campaign starts to feel like it has a spine. I didn't plan the guild-sigil connection in advance — it came from combining two things the players had already found. Keep your clues generic enough that you can wire them together after the fact.
